About

The alchemist behind the polish.

hi! 👋

Hi, I'm Ella, I started Nail Alchemist after years of doing nails for friends at the kitchen table, mixing polish shades until they matched a mood rather than a chart. Vienna is small enough that I still remember most of the hands that walk through the door.

I trained in classic manicure and gel technique, then kept adding what I actually use: freehand nail art, color theory, and a lot of patience for people who "don't really know what they want" and that's most of my favorite appointments.

🎨
The approach

How I work

One hour, no rush

Every appointment is booked with enough room to actually finish a design properly, even a fiddly one.

Your nails come first

If a shape or a product isn't right for your nails, I'll say so, even if it's not what you asked for.

Color, considered

I keep a working library of mixed shades and swatches, bring a photo, a fabric scrap, anything, and we'll find (or mix) the match.
